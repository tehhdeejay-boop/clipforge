Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Who uses Clipforge

Clipforge is used only by its owner, Dylan Hawkins, on his own computer to publish to his own accounts. It has no other users and no website sign-up.

What data Clipforge accesses

  • TikTok basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope, to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to publish videos via video.publish and video.upload, to post clips the owner has approved.

How data is stored

The TikTok access token is stored only on the owner's computer. Clipforge has no server and no database outside that computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions, which immediately stops Clipforge from posting.
