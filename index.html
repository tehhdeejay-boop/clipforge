Clipforge

Clipforge is a personal desktop tool used by the creator of the balhoggyclips accounts. It turns long-form videos into short vertical clips with captions, lets the creator review and approve each clip, and then publishes approved clips to the creator's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

How it works

  1. The creator adds a video to Clipforge on their own computer.
  2. Clipforge finds highlight moments and renders vertical clips with captions.
  3. The creator reviews every clip, edits the title and caption, and approves it.
  4. The creator connects their TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit, and Clipforge publishes the approved clip to that account using the TikTok Content Posting API.

Clipforge is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.
